How it works

Tap the map to start, then tap again to route to each next point. Press Draw and trace with your finger or mouse to pick exact roads; the line snaps to roads and park connectors. Trace over part of a route to replace it. The eraser removes what you rub out and joins the gap. Turn Snap off to draw straight lines anywhere. While drawing, move the map with a right-drag, by holding Space and dragging, or with two fingers on a phone.

Routing uses named streets (OpenStreetMap, expressways left out) and NParks park connectors, drawn on centrelines. Unnamed footpaths are not in the network yet. Elevation comes from a 90 m terrain model, so treat it as a guide.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; park connectors © NParks via data.gov.sg; elevation Open-Meteo.
